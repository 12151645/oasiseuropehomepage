Typography system and font rules for Oasis Europe site

## Fonts (ONLY TWO)
- **Lora** (`font-display`) → H1 and H2 headings only, weight 500–600, tracking -0.01em
- **Source Sans Pro** (`font-body`) → everything else: body, labels, buttons, nav, cards, h3+

## Removed fonts
- Cormorant Garamond — removed entirely
- Source Sans 3 — replaced by Source Sans Pro

## Typography classes
- `heading-xl`: Lora, medium weight, text-4xl to text-6xl
- `heading-lg`: Lora, medium weight, text-3xl to text-5xl
- `heading-md`: Source Sans Pro, medium weight, text-xl to text-2xl
- `body-lg`: Source Sans Pro, light, text-base to text-lg, line-height 1.7
- `body-md`: Source Sans Pro, light, text-sm to text-base, line-height 1.7
- `label-sm`: Source Sans Pro, medium, text-xs, uppercase, tracking 0.08em
